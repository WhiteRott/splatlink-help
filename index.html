Help and support

SplatLink connects your Android phone to your PC. You capture with the phone. Your PC builds the Gaussian splat.

SplatLink is in development. Some features on this page are not available yet. We update this page as the app changes.

What you need

Phone

  • An Android phone with a good rear camera.
  • Enough free storage for video.

PC

  • Windows, or Ubuntu 24.04 Linux.
  • A GPU with 8 GB of video memory or more is best.
  • An NVIDIA GPU gives the fastest results.
  • AMD and Intel GPUs also work for training.
  • Free disk space for images and project files.

Network

  • The phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • A fast network makes uploads quicker.

Capture tips

Good capture matters more than any setting. Follow these steps for the best splat.

Walk-around capture (recommended)

  1. Choose an object with texture. Avoid shiny, clear or plain white objects.
  2. Use even light. Do not use the flash.
  3. Walk slowly around the object in one full circle.
  4. Keep the object in the center of the frame.
  5. Do a second circle from a higher angle.
  6. Record for 30 to 60 seconds.

Turntable capture (coming later)

  • Put a plain backdrop behind and under the object.
  • Cover the turntable top with the same plain material.
  • Fix the phone on a tripod. Lock focus and exposure.
  • Do one turn from the side and one from a higher angle.

Common problems

The splat is blurry or has floating specks
  • Move more slowly during capture.
  • Add more light, and keep it even.
  • Clean the camera lens.
  • Try a higher quality setting.
  • Remove specks with the crop and delete tools in Preview.
Camera tracking failed, or only part of the object appears
  • Make sure each part of the object shows in many frames.
  • Avoid plain or shiny surfaces. Tracking needs texture.
  • On a turntable, the background must be plain. A busy background confuses tracking.
  • Capture again with more overlap between views.
The PC uses the wrong GPU
  • Open the settings, and select your fastest GPU by name.
  • A PC with more than one GPU can list them in any order. The first GPU is not always the fastest.
The PC runs out of video memory
  • Choose a lower quality setting.
  • Use a shorter video, or fewer images.
  • Close other programs that use the GPU, such as games.
Linux: the NVIDIA GPU does not appear
  • Install the NVIDIA driver from your Linux distribution.
  • Check that Vulkan sees the GPU. Run vulkaninfo --summary.
  • If you run SplatLink in a container, make sure the container can reach the NVIDIA driver.
The phone cannot find the PC
  • Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Make sure SplatLink is open on the PC.
  • Allow SplatLink through the PC firewall.

Questions

Do my photos and videos go to the cloud?

No. Your PC does the processing. Your files go from your phone to your own PC.

Which file formats can I export?

You can export standard PLY, compressed PLY, SPZ and SOG files. These work in most splat viewers and editors.

How long does processing take?

It depends on your GPU, the quality setting and the length of the video. Most objects take between a few minutes and half an hour.

Report an issue

Tell us about bugs and problems. Include these details so we can help faster:

  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • Your PC operating system and GPU.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  • The error message or log, if there is one.
  • A screenshot, if it helps.
