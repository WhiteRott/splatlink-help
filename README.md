# SplatLink Help

> [!WARNING]
> **PC performance notice**
>
> SplatLink uses your PC's graphics card (GPU). We recommend a GPU with 8 GB of video memory or more. NVIDIA cards give the fastest results. Speed and quality depend on your hardware. Older PCs and PCs with less video memory take longer and may not run the high-quality settings.
>
> **Use the free 3-day trial to test SplatLink on your PC before you buy.**
